Cards are printed through the printer's own Windows driver, so the
printer's driver must be installed first. Each card template has its card
size: CR80 (85.6 x 54 mm, the usual ID card), CR79 (83.9 x 52.1 mm), CR100
(98.5 x 67 mm) or a custom size, and the printer must accept that size. Cards
are sent at the printer's own resolution: 600 dpi for 600 dpi printers,
otherwise 300 dpi. How much the software can report about the printer depends
on its driver.


HID FARGO PRINTERS
Printing, live status and alerts on every model. Ribbon and film levels, the
card counter and flipper (dual-sided) detection are read from the FARGO
driver and shown when the driver reports them; film levels only apply to
retransfer (HDP) models. The software was developed against the HDP5000 and
checked on Windows 11; the other models use the same FARGO driver family but
have not all been tested with this software.

BASIC SUPPORT - OTHER CARD PRINTERS
Any card printer with a Windows driver for the card size used, for example
Zebra, Evolis, Magicard, Entrust (Datacard) and IDP printers. Printing works
through the driver, and alerts are limited to what Windows reports (offline,
paper/card out, jams, errors). Ribbon levels are not shown.


DUAL-SIDED PRINTING
Printers with a flipper module print both sides in one pass. Printers
without one can still make dual-sided cards: print all the fronts, flip the
stack, then press "Print backs" in the print queue (Settings > Printing >
How the back is printed > Manually).


NOT SUPPORTED
Ordinary office and photo printers, label printers, and network print
services that do not use a Windows card-printer driver.
